A Tuscan & Lombardy Escape

Florence • Milan • Lake Como | Sept 22 - 25, 2026

Renaissance & Sunsets

Your journey begins in the heart of Tuscany.

Afternoon: Arrival & Check-in

Fly BER to LIN, take the high-speed train to Florence. Check into your 6-bed mixed dorm at PLUS Florence Hostel, featuring excellent social spaces and a quick walk to the center.
Dorm Bed: ~€50 - €60 (1 night)

Late Afternoon: Grand Architecture

Walk through the beautiful alleys toward the Cathedral of Santa Maria del Fiore (The Duomo) to marvel at its incredible marble facade and dome.

Golden Hour: The Perfect Sketch

Cross the Arno river and hike up to Piazzale Michelangelo. This terraced piazza offers the most iconic panoramic sunset views over Florence—the perfect spot to bring out your watercolor markers.

Fine Art to High Fashion

Transitioning from Tuscan history to Lombardy's elegance.

Morning: Masters of the Renaissance

Dive into the Uffizi Galleries early to study the composition and techniques of Botticelli and da Vinci's masterpieces.

Afternoon: High-Speed to Milan

Train back north to Milan. Check into your 6-bed dorm at the lively Ostello Bello Grande right by the Central Station for fast transit.
Dorm Bed: ~€35 - €40/night (2 nights)

Evening: Canals & Architecture

Walk through the glass-vaulted Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II. Later, head to the Cerchia dei Navigli (Navigli District) for a sunset aperitivo.

A Lakeside Retreat

Finding peace, quiet, and creative inspiration by the water.

Morning: Train to Varenna

Take the scenic 1-hour train from Milan Central to Varenna Esino on the shores of Lake Como. Walk the "Passeggiata degli Innamorati" (Lover's Path) along the water.

Afternoon: Villa Monastero

Spend the afternoon in the botanical gardens of Villa Monastero. Its terraced waterfront statues and cypress trees provide a secluded environment for meditation and painting.

Evening: Return to Milan

Catch the local train back to Milan for your final night at Ostello Bello Grande.

Auf Wiedersehen

A quick espresso before heading back to reality.

Morning: Final Breakfast

Enjoy breakfast at the hostel, reviewing your sketches and notes from the trip.

Afternoon: Flight to Berlin

Transit to Linate Airport for the easyJet flight back to Berlin Brandenburg Airport.
